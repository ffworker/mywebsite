LLMs And Self-hosted Agents

I use agents openly, not magically

I do not hide that LLMs help me. I also do not confuse generated output with verified infrastructure.

How I use them well

  • Break vague goals into inspectable steps.
  • Generate drafts, scripts, manifests, and dashboards faster.
  • Compare output against live systems, logs, metrics, and Git diffs.
  • Keep secrets out of repos and treat production changes carefully.

What still stays human

  • Choosing the operational tradeoff.
  • Understanding what should not be deleted.
  • Verifying that a change actually works.
  • Admitting gaps and turning them into practice plans.
